DAUS Disaster-Response Drone

One of four paper authorsPublished · DAUS 2026
▧
DAUS Disaster-Response DroneProject imagery

THE BUILD

An autonomous drone concept for scanning an area, detecting disaster zones with YOLOv8 and delivering a payload without continuous ground control.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • Built heading-aware pixel-to-GPS conversion onboard.
  • Combined LiDAR, PX4 telemetry and GPS to reconstruct terrain and fly lower.
  • A Raspberry Pi 5 sent waypoints to a Pixhawk 4 through pymavlink; Wi-Fi Direct supported peer-to-peer communication.
  • The paper reports SITL and field testing, including a 2 kg payload and 25-minute endurance.
