04 / Engineering approach

HOW I WORK.

A practical set of principles for building robotics systems that are understandable, testable and safer to operate.

01

Make interfaces explicit

Keep perception, reasoning, planning and actuation separated by clear interfaces. A failure should be traceable to a layer, not hidden inside a monolith.

02

Treat autonomy as a safety problem

Validate commands before they reach motion systems. Bound inputs, reject malformed actions and keep deterministic checks between language models and robot control.

03

Debug the cause, not the symptom

Reproduce the issue, inspect logs and lifecycle states, then fix the dependency or timing problem instead of piling on retries.

04

Test the complete loop

A node that starts is not a working robot. Test the flow from sensing or voice input through planning to actuation, and distinguish simulation results from hardware validation.
