12-DOF Quadruped

Personal projectBuilt and tested

12-DOF QuadrupedProject imagery

THE BUILD

A compact quadruped using an Arduino Nano and twelve SG90 servos for walking and gestures such as waving and tilting.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • Used three degrees of freedom per leg for articulated movement.
  • Implemented predefined gait sequences for repeatable motion.
  • Added Bluetooth control with an HC-05 module.
